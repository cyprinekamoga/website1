Our Story

From Our Kitchen to Yours

JAGAA's Munchies started with a simple idea: the snacks we grew up loving — warm mandazi, crispy samosas, soft chapati and a proper Rolex — should be easy to get, and should always taste homemade.

That's why we never cook in bulk and leave things waiting. Every donut, samosa and chapati is made fresh after you order, so it reaches you the way it's meant to be enjoyed.

— JAGAA's Munchies
